Government of Andhra Pradesh have constituted Andhra Pradesh Information Commission to exercise
the powers conferred on and to perform the functions assigned to it under Right to Information
Act, 2005.
Andhra Pradesh Information Commission as a quasi-judical body decides the Complaints and Second
Appeals filed under Right to Information Act, 2005. The jurisdiction of the Commission extends
over all Andhra Pradesh State Public Authorities.
